A friendship memory · Never forgotten

A Friend Like Saim

Ek din teacher ne homework ki assignment 8 martaba likhne ko di. Haseeb se assignment nahi ho saki, lekin Saim ne apna kaam poora kiya hua tha. Jab teacher assignment check karne aaye aur Haseeb ne Saim ko sach bataya, toh Saim ne apni 4 martaba wali assignment usay de di.

Phir jab teacher ne saza ki baat ki, Saim ne Haseeb ko akela nahi chhora. Usne teacher se kaha ke Haseeb ko na marein, uske hisse ki saza bhi mujhe de dein. Us lamhe ne Haseeb ke dil mein ek aisi yaad bana di jo aaj tak zinda hai.

"Us din mujhe samajh aaya ke dosti sirf saath hansne ka naam nahi. Dosti ka matlab hai mushkil waqt mein bhi ek doosre ka saath na chhorna."

Shayad Saim ko woh din yaad ho ya na ho, lekin Haseeb ke liye woh waqya hamesha khaas rahega. 11 saal ki dosti mein aise hi chhote lamhe bohat badi yaadein ban jaate hain.

— Haseeb, dil se

Letters for My Friends

Har dost ke liye dil se ek chhota sa paighaam.

Dear Saim ❤️

11 saal ki dosti mein tumhare saath bohat se lamhe guzre. Homework wala woh din main kabhi nahi bhool sakta. Tumne us waqt mera saath diya aur mujhe dosti ka matlab mehsoos karwaya. Shukriya ke tum meri kahani ka itna khaas hissa ho.

Dear Abdullah

Yaar, purane din yaad aate hain. Woh saath baithna, baatein karna aur chhoti chhoti shararatein — kaash kabhi phir sab ek jagah hon.

Dear Sahil

Jab humne purane waqt ko yaad kiya aur tum bhi emotional ho gaye, mujhe mehsoos hua ke woh yaadein sirf mere liye nahi thin. Hamari dosti ke woh lamhe hamesha khaas rahenge.

Dear Ayan

Hamari dosti ke woh din, woh hansi mazaak aur saath guzara waqt yaad aata hai. Umeed hai ek din phir sab dost saath baith kar purani baatein karenge.

Dear Hussnain

Tum bhi un yaadon ka hissa ho jo main dil se sambhal kar rakhta hoon. Zindagi jahan bhi le jaye, purane doston ki jagah khaas rehti hai.

To all of us

Hum sab ki apni apni zindagi hai, lekin hamari kahani ka ek hissa hamesha wohi rahega: ek saath guzare hue din. Jab bhi milo, purani dosti ke saath milna.

One wish, from the heart

Kaash Woh Din Phir Laut Aayein

Waqt ko wapas nahi laa sakte, lekin ek doosre se dobara milne ka din zaroor bana sakte hain.
